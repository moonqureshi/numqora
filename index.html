How to Calculate Percentage

A percentage shows a number as a fraction of 100. Percentages are commonly used for discounts, marks, prices, business calculations and many other situations.

Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

Example

If you scored 45 marks out of 60:

(45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%

Percentage Increase Formula

((New Value - Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100
