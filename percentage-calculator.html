How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage expresses a number as a part of 100. Percentages are useful for shopping discounts, exam marks, business profits, price changes and everyday calculations.

Percentage Formula

Percentage of a number: (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

What percentage: (Part ÷ Total) × 100

Percentage change: ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Examples

20% of 500 is 100.

25 is 25% of 100.

An increase from 100 to 125 is a 25% increase.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 10% of a number?

Divide the number by 10. For example, 10% of 200 is 20.

How do I calculate a percentage discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage divided by 100. Subtract the result from the original price.
